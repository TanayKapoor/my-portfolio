### 3. Compact Spotlight & All Events

Instead of two separate full tables:
* Spotlight = **top N rows of filtered table** + small “View All” link
* All Filtered Events = same table, just longer.
* This removes duplication and reduces scrolling.

⠀
⸻

### 4. Use Visual Hierarchy Instead of Big Colors
* Use **icons + small badges** for risk level in table rows instead of full red background cells.
* Highlight critical rows with a **left border** in the risk color, not a solid fill.

⠀

### 5. Keep Project Status Always Visible

Add a **persistent slim header bar** above the dashboard that shows:

Mode: Local | Last run: 2025-08-10 10:18 UTC | ROC-AUC: 0.848 | PR-AUC: 0.743

* This keeps performance and freshness in view at all times without taking a whole card.
